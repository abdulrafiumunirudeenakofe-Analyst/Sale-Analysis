An end-to-end sales performance, customer segmentation, and revenue leakage analysis built using advanced Excel (Pivot Tables, Custom Measures, and Interactive Dashboards). This project evaluates company-wide performance for **Apex Commerce**, identifying top-performing assets, regional trends, and critical areas of financial loss.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions Addressed

* **What is driving revenue change?** Monitored monthly trends showing a peak performance period between July and October, followed by a steep end-of-year adjustment.
* **Which regions and categories lead?** **Asia Pacific** leads regional sales ($177K), while **Electronics** dominates product categories generating over $266K in revenue.
* **Where are sales growing or declining?** Evaluated channel traction across Direct B2B, Retail Partners, Marketplaces, and Online platforms.
* **How much business is being lost?** Uncovered **$165,602 in Revenue at Risk**, driven by $88,373 in refunds and $77,229 in cancellations alongside a notable **29% failed order rate**.

## 🛠️ Built With & Methodology

* **Microsoft Excel:** 
  * **Data Modeling & Pivot Tables:** Aggregating raw sales records to slice data dynamically by region, category, channel, and timeframe.
  * **Custom Measures & Formulas:** Utilizing advanced calculations for order success ratios, revenue loss metrics, and customer rankings.
  * **Interactive Dashboards:** Designed with interconnected slicers and multi-tab navigation to transition seamlessly between executive summaries, product insights, and risk assessments.
